Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

ASTM Engineering ("we") uses an internal tool, "ASTM Reels Uploader", to publish our own company videos to our YouTube, Instagram and Threads accounts. This policy explains what data that tool uses.

What we access

The tool signs in only with ASTM Engineering's own Google account and requests permission to upload videos to our own YouTube channel (YouTube Data API, upload scope). It reads videos only from a Google Drive folder that we created and shared with it.

What we do not do

The tool is not offered to the public. It does not collect, store, sell or share personal data of any other user, and it does not access YouTube or Google data beyond uploading our own videos.

Storage and security

The access token is stored as an encrypted secret in our private build system and is used only to upload videos we have approved. We can revoke access at any time from our Google account settings (myaccount.google.com/permissions).

Google API Services

Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Uploaded videos are subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
